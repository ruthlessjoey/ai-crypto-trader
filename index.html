Flex Signal Lab

For your Olymptrade Flex chart. Enter recent observed prices to get a transparent technical signal. This app never logs in, connects to Olymptrade, or places orders.

Important: This is a rule-based prototype, not a trained AI model or a reliable predictor. Five-second trades are extremely noisy. A signal can be wrong, and a high displayed score is not a probability of profit. Use demo/paper tracking only while validating it.

1 · Enter recent prices

Copy recent price values from the chart, oldest first and newest last, separated by commas. Use at least 15 values; 30–60 is better. Do not enter a future price.

2 · Signal result

Your result appears here after analysis.

3 · What the signal means

  • BUY candidate: short EMA is above long EMA and recent momentum/RSI filters agree.
  • SELL candidate: short EMA is below long EMA and momentum/RSI filters agree.
  • WAIT: signals conflict, data is insufficient, or the setup is too weak.

The score is a transparent rule score, not a machine-learning probability. This prototype uses price-only indicators because it has no verified feed for the specific Asia Composite Index.

4 · Paper-trade log

Record what happened after a signal. This helps evaluate the strategy before risking money.

This measures direction only; it doesn't calculate platform payout, fees, or actual profitability.
